MOE Primary Science Syllabus - Level P6
华文: 科学 P6
Source: MOE Primary School Syllabus (public Crown Copyright, paraphrased)

Topic: Food Chains and Food Webs (食物链和食物网)
Code: SCI-P6-001
  Learning Outcome 1: Construct food chains and food webs
  Learning Outcome 2: Identify producers, consumers and decomposers

Topic: Adaptation to Environment (对环境的适应)
Code: SCI-P6-002
  Learning Outcome 1: Describe how structural and behavioural adaptations help organisms survive
  Learning Outcome 2: Explain the relationship between adaptation and habitat

Topic: Electric Circuits (电路)
Code: SCI-P6-003
  Learning Outcome 1: Draw and interpret circuit diagrams
  Learning Outcome 2: Describe how switches control circuits
  Learning Outcome 3: Identify series and parallel circuits

Topic: Water Cycle (水循环)
Code: SCI-P6-004
  Learning Outcome 1: Describe the processes of evaporation, condensation and precipitation
  Learning Outcome 2: Explain the water cycle as a continuous process

Topic: Forces (力)
Code: SCI-P6-005
  Learning Outcome 1: Identify types of forces (friction, gravity, elastic spring, magnetic)
  Learning Outcome 2: Describe how forces affect motion

Topic: PSLE Science Application (小六会考科学应用)
Code: SCI-P6-006
  Learning Outcome 1: Apply scientific concepts to novel contexts
  Learning Outcome 2: Design fair tests and identify variables
  Learning Outcome 3: Draw conclusions from experimental data

Topic: Photosynthesis (光合作用)
Code: SCI-P6-007
  Learning Outcome 1: Describe the process of photosynthesis
  Learning Outcome 2: Identify conditions necessary for photosynthesis

Topic: Human Body Systems (人体系统)
Code: SCI-P6-008
  Learning Outcome 1: Describe how body systems work together
  Learning Outcome 2: Explain how the nervous system coordinates responses
