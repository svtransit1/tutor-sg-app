MOE Primary English Language Syllabus - Level P6
华文: 英语 P6
Source: MOE Primary School Syllabus (public Crown Copyright, paraphrased)

Topic: PSLE Reading Comprehension (小六会考阅读理解)
Code: ENG-P6-001
  Learning Outcome 1: Answer open-ended comprehension questions with text evidence
  Learning Outcome 2: Analyse writer's techniques (figurative language, structure)

Topic: PSLE Vocabulary (小六会考词汇)
Code: ENG-P6-002
  Learning Outcome 1: Demonstrate mastery of PSLE vocabulary list
  Learning Outcome 2: Use vocabulary-in-context MCQ strategies

Topic: PSLE Grammar (小六会考语法)
Code: ENG-P6-003
  Learning Outcome 1: Demonstrate mastery of all primary school grammar structures
  Learning Outcome 2: Edit passages for grammatical accuracy

Topic: PSLE Composition (小六会考作文)
Code: ENG-P6-004
  Learning Outcome 1: Write a well-structured narrative (150-200 words) with plot, character development and resolution
  Learning Outcome 2: Use varied sentence structures and vocabulary

Topic: PSLE Oral Examination (小六会考口试)
Code: ENG-P6-005
  Learning Outcome 1: Read aloud with confidence, accuracy and expression
  Learning Outcome 2: Engage in sustained stimulus-based conversation

Topic: PSLE Listening Comprehension (小六会考听力理解)
Code: ENG-P6-006
  Learning Outcome 1: Understand spoken texts and answer MCQ and open-ended questions
  Learning Outcome 2: Identify speaker attitudes and infer meaning

Topic: PSLE Writing (Functional) (小六会考应用文写作)
Code: ENG-P6-007
  Learning Outcome 1: Write formal and informal emails/letters
  Learning Outcome 2: Write speeches, reports and reviews with appropriate format
