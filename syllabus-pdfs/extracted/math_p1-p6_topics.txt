MOE Primary Mathematics Syllabus - Level P6
华文: 数学 P6
Source: MOE Primary School Syllabus (public Crown Copyright, paraphrased)

Topic: Numbers and Operations (数和运算)
Code: MAT-P6-001
  Learning Outcome 1: Perform mixed operations with fractions, decimals and percentages
  Learning Outcome 2: Solve complex word problems

Topic: Ratio, Percentage and Rate (比、百分比和速率)
Code: MAT-P6-002
  Learning Outcome 1: Solve multi-step problems involving ratio, percentage and rate
  Learning Outcome 2: Apply to real-world contexts like discounts and GST

Topic: Algebra (代数)
Code: MAT-P6-003
  Learning Outcome 1: Simplify algebraic expressions
  Learning Outcome 2: Solve linear equations with one unknown
  Learning Outcome 3: Substitute values

Topic: Geometry (几何)
Code: MAT-P6-004
  Learning Outcome 1: Identify nets of 3D solids
  Learning Outcome 2: Calculate angles on a straight line and at a point
  Learning Outcome 3: Use properties of special triangles

Topic: Area and Volume (面积和体积)
Code: MAT-P6-005
  Learning Outcome 1: Find area of triangles and composite shapes
  Learning Outcome 2: Calculate volume of cubes and cuboids in practical contexts

Topic: Speed (速度)
Code: MAT-P6-006
  Learning Outcome 1: Use the relationship distance = speed × time
  Learning Outcome 2: Solve word problems involving speed

Topic: Data Analysis (数据分析)
Code: MAT-P6-007
  Learning Outcome 1: Read and interpret pie charts
  Learning Outcome 2: Solve problems from tables, graphs and charts

Topic: PSLE Problem Solving (小六会考解题)
Code: MAT-P6-008
  Learning Outcome 1: Apply heuristic strategies: guess and check, before-after, working backwards
  Learning Outcome 2: Solve multi-step non-routine problems
